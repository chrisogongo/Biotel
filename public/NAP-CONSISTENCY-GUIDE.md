# NAP Consistency Guide for Biotel DNA

## What is NAP?

NAP stands for **Name, Address, Phone number**. Consistency in NAP across all online platforms is crucial for local SEO. Search engines use NAP consistency to verify business legitimacy and improve local search rankings.

## Common NAP Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Don't Do This:

1. **Inconsistent Phone Formats**
   - ❌ Using +254 711 371092 on one page and 0711 371092 on another
   - ✅ Use +254 711 371092 consistently (or choose one format and stick to it)

2. **Business Name Variations**
   - ❌ "Biotel DNA" on website, "Biotel DNA Kenya" on Facebook
   - ✅ Use "Biotel DNA" everywhere

3. **Missing or Fake Addresses**
   - ❌ Publishing a fake street address
   - ✅ Only publish verified addresses, or use city-level location

4. **Outdated Information**
   - ❌ Old phone number still listed on directories
   - ✅ Update all platforms when information changes

5. **Inconsistent Hours**
   - ❌ Different hours on website vs Google Business Profile
   - ✅ Keep hours consistent across all platforms
