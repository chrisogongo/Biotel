**Q: Do you offer paternity DNA testing?**
A: Yes, we provide professional paternity DNA testing services in Kenya. Tests are available as private (peace of mind) or legal tests for court purposes. Contact us at +254 711 371092 for more information.

**Q: Can I get tested in Mombasa or Kisumu?**

**Q: Is DNA testing confidential?**
A: Yes, all our DNA testing services are completely confidential. Results are shared only with authorised individuals and never disclosed to third parties without consent.

**Q: How much does a DNA test cost?**
A: DNA test costs vary depending on the test type, number of participants, and whether you need a private or legal test. Contact us at +254 711 371092 for a personalised quotation.

**Q: Do you offer home sample collection?**
A: Yes, we offer home DNA sample collection within Nairobi. Our trained collectors come to your preferred location. Contact us to arrange home collection.

**Q: How long does it take to get results?**
A: Turnaround times vary depending on the test type and current workload. Contact us for current estimated turnaround times for your specific test.

**Q: What's the difference between private and legal DNA tests?**
A: Private tests are for personal knowledge only. Legal tests follow chain-of-custody procedures with formal identification, making results suitable for court and official purposes.

## 7. Reviews Management

### Review Collection Strategy

#### Encourage Reviews
- Ask satisfied clients to leave Google reviews
- Provide direct review link
- Send follow-up messages after service
- Include review request in email signatures

#### Review Link Format
```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID
```

#### Response Guidelines

**Positive Reviews:**
- Thank the reviewer
- Mention specific details from their review
- Invite them to contact for future needs
- Keep response professional and warm

**Example Response:**
```
Thank you for your kind review, [Name]! We're glad we could help you with your DNA testing needs. Our team is committed to providing professional, confidential service. Please don't hesitate to contact us if you need any assistance in the future.

Best regards,
Biotel DNA Team
```

**Negative Reviews:**
- Respond within 24-48 hours
- Stay professional and calm
- Acknowledge their concern
- Offer to resolve privately
- Don't argue or get defensive
- Take conversation offline

**Example Response:**
```
Dear [Name],

Thank you for your feedback. We're sorry to hear about your experience and take your concerns seriously. We'd like to understand more about what happened and work to resolve this.

Please contact us directly at +254 711 371092 or email info@bioteldna.co.ke so we can discuss this privately.

Best regards,
Biotel DNA Team
```

### Review Goals

- **3 Months:** 10+ reviews, 4.5+ average rating
- **6 Months:** 25+ reviews, 4.5+ average rating
- **12 Months:** 50+ reviews, 4.5+ average rating
