Frequently Asked Questions

How does a DNA paternity test work?
A DNA paternity test compares the genetic markers of a child with those of the alleged father. Since a child inherits half of their DNA from each parent, the test can determine with a very high degree of accuracy whether the tested man is the biological father. Sample collection is simple — usually a buccal (cheek) swab — and the process is painless and non-invasive.
How much does a DNA test cost in Kenya?

How long does it take to get DNA test results?
Turnaround times vary depending on the type of test and current laboratory workload. Generally, results are available within a few working days after samples are received at the laboratory. Contact us for current estimated turnaround times for your specific test.
Is DNA testing confidential?
Yes. Biotel DNA takes confidentiality very seriously. All test information, results, and personal details are handled with strict privacy protocols. Results are shared only with authorised individuals and are never disclosed to third parties without consent.
What is the difference between a private and a legal DNA test?
A private DNA test (also called a peace of mind test) is for personal knowledge only and does not follow formal identification procedures. A legal DNA test follows strict chain-of-custody procedures, with formal identification of all participants and authorised sample collection, making the results suitable for court or official purposes.
Can a DNA test be done without the mother's participation?
Yes. A paternity DNA test can be conducted with just the child and the alleged father. However, including the mother's sample can strengthen the accuracy and conclusiveness of the results. If the mother is unavailable, the test can still provide reliable results.
