Private vs Legal DNA Testing in Kenya

Understanding the difference between private and legal DNA testing helps you choose the right option for your needs.

Both types use the same scientific analysis — the difference lies in the procedures surrounding sample collection and documentation.

🔒 Private DNA Test

Also called a "peace of mind" test.

  • ✓ For personal knowledge only
  • ✓ No formal ID required
  • ✓ Simple sample collection
  • ✓ Results NOT admissible in court
  • ✓ Generally more affordable

⚖️ Legal DNA Test

For court, immigration, and official purposes.

  • ✓ Court-admissible results
  • ✓ Formal ID verification required
  • ✓ Chain-of-custody procedures
  • ✓ Authorised sample collection
  • ✓ Full documentation provided

Which Do You Need?

Choose a private test if you want personal knowledge for your own peace of mind.

Choose a legal test if you need results for court proceedings, child custody, immigration, or inheritance disputes.

If you're unsure, contact Biotel DNA for guidance.
